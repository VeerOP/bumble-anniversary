# Bumble • 1 Year Anniversary 💛🐝

A cute, interactive Bumble-themed swiping website celebrating 1 year of meeting on Bumble.

## ✨ Features
- **Authentic Bumble Swiping Experience**: Card stack with 13 milestone memories, authentic prompts, basics badges, and Spotify anthem.
- **Playful Anti-Rejection**: Swiping left or clicking ✕ playfully bounces back into the center with hilarious alerts.
- **Bumble Action Dock**: Rewind (↺), Pass (✕), SuperSwipe (⭐️), Like (💛), Compliment (💬).
- **Celebration Finale**: Confetti explosion, overlapping glowing avatars with 365-day beating heart, and milestone counters.
- **Interactive Modals**:
  - 💌 Wax-sealed handwritten love letter with a "Send Her A Kiss 💋" button.
  - 💬 Bumble chat history replica with voice note simulation and live replies.
  - 📸 Polaroid memory gallery featuring all 13 photos.
- **Web Audio Sound & Music**: Tones for swipes, chimes for celebration, and a gentle synthesized romantic melody.
- **1-Click Personalization**: Easily customize names, date, and messages.
